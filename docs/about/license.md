# License

MESA Trainings has two licenses, one for the teaching material and one for code.

| What | License | Full text |
|---|---|---|
| Training content: the tutorials, the other pages of this site, and their images | [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)[^cc-by] | [`LICENSE`](https://github.com/idss-mesa/trainings/blob/main/LICENSE) |
| Code: the scripts that build the site, and code samples in the tutorials | BSD 3-Clause, as in the other MESA repositories | [`LICENSE-CODE`](https://github.com/idss-mesa/trainings/blob/main/LICENSE-CODE) |

Copyright © 2026 The Regents of the University of New Mexico[^repo].

## Reusing a tutorial

You may copy, share, and adapt the training content for any purpose, as long as you give
credit, link to the license, and say whether you changed anything. An attribution line
such as this is enough:

```text
"<Tutorial title>" by the MESA project (https://idss-mesa.github.io/trainings/),
licensed CC BY 4.0. <Adapted from the original. | Unchanged.>
```

## Material from elsewhere

Screenshots of the MESA Portal are shown from the
[MESA documentation](https://idss-mesa.github.io/docs/) and belong to that project. A
tutorial that uses a dataset carries that dataset's own attribution line.
