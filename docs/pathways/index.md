# Learning pathways

* [New to MESA](new-to-mesa.md) - Sign in, find data, run a notebook, and keep what you make: a route of 6 tutorials that takes 30 to 40 minutes in all.
* [Get ready for a hands-on session](get-ready-for-a-session.md) - Arrive able to sign in, run a notebook, check a dataset, and leave work that reruns: a route of 13 tutorials that takes 70 to 90 minutes in all.
* [Check a dataset before you trust it](check-a-dataset.md) - Find out what a dataset holds and where it is incomplete or doubtful: a route of 7 tutorials that takes 35 to 45 minutes in all.
* [Work in R](work-in-r.md) - Do the data checks in MESA RStudio Geospatial: a route of 4 tutorials that takes 30 to 35 minutes in all.
* [Use an AI agent and check what it says](use-an-ai-agent.md) - Start an agent in a MESA app and test its answers against the data: a route of 6 tutorials that takes 40 to 45 minutes in all.
* [Leave work others can rerun](leave-work-others-can-rerun.md) - Describe, record, and share work so that someone else can run it again: a route of 5 tutorials that takes 30 to 35 minutes in all.
* [Every tutorial, by pathway point](../pathway.md) - Every MESA tutorial under the five points of the learning pathway, from finding your way around to sharing work others can rerun, with the time each takes.
* [Self-checks](self-checks.md) - Short questions for checking yourself after a tutorial, each with its answer, the likely slips, and how the answer is confirmed against the practice data.
* [Your progress](../progress.md) - Where you are on each learning pathway, kept only in your own browser, with ways to save a copy to a file, bring one back, and clear it.
