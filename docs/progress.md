# Your progress

This page shows where you are on each [learning pathway](pathways/index.md): what you
have finished, what comes next, and how far along each of the five points you are.

It is drawn by a script in your browser. If you see only this text, scripts are off
or blocked. The pathways and the tutorials work without them; only the record of what
you have finished is missing.

## How your progress is kept

Your progress is kept in this browser only. Nothing is sent to MESA or to anyone else,
and no account is involved.

- **"Done" is your own word.** The tutorials happen in the MESA Portal, in another tab,
  and this site cannot see them. A tutorial is marked done when you say so.
- **A browser can clear it.** Safari does so after seven days without a visit[^webkit-itp], and
  clearing your browsing history does it in any browser. Progress made in one browser
  or on one device does not appear in another.
- **Save a copy if you want to keep it.** With scripts on, this page offers to save
  your progress to a file, to bring a saved file back, and to clear everything.

## What the record holds

For each tutorial you finish: its name, the day you marked it done, and which revision
of the tutorial that was, so the site can tell you if the tutorial has changed since.
It also holds the pathway you are following and, if you choose one, the level you give
yourself at each point.

If you use the [self-checks](pathways/self-checks.md), it holds for each question
whether you got it right, how many tries you took, how sure you said you were, and the
day. If you write a reflection at the end of a point, it holds your words. You can save
your reflections, levels, and finished tutorials as a Markdown file to bring with you.

It holds nothing about who you are.
