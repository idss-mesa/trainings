# Find your way around the MESA Portal

!!! warning "Draft: not yet validated"
    This tutorial was written from the MESA documentation. Nobody has yet followed it
    from start to finish on the live portal, so a screen name or a step may differ.
    [Tell us what you find](https://github.com/idss-mesa/trainings/issues).

**You will:** know where each part of the portal is, and have checked how much of your
quota you have used.

**Time:** 5 minutes. **You need:** to be [signed in to the MESA Portal](sign-in.md).

## Steps

1. Click **Home** in the sidebar on the left.

    You should see: your dashboard, with four panels.

    ![The portal dashboard: Recent Analyses, Resource Usage, My Working Groups, and Quick Actions](https://idss-mesa.github.io/docs/assets/portal/portal-dashboard.webp)

2. Read **Resource Usage**.

    You should see: CPU hours and Data Store space, each as an amount used against your
    CyVerse quota.

3. Read down the sidebar.

    You should see: three groups. **Portal Navigation** holds **Home**, **Events**, and
    **Working Groups**. **Data & Apps** holds **Data Browser**, **Applications**, and
    **Analyses**. **Help & Docs** holds **Documentation**.

4. Click **Data Browser**, then **Applications**, then **Analyses**, looking at each
   page before you move on.

    You should see: your files; a card for each MESA app; and a list of the apps you
    have run, which is empty in a new account.

5. Look at the right-hand end of the top bar.

    You should see: a round theme button, and your name, which opens your user menu.

6. Click the theme button until the page suits you.

    You should see: the portal change between **Light**, **Dark**, and **System**.
    System follows your computer's setting.

7. Click your name.

    You should see: **My Profile**, **Settings**, and **Logout**.

## Check it worked

From any page you can reach your files, the apps, and your analyses in one click, and
you can say roughly how many CPU hours you have left.

## If it didn't

**There is no sidebar.** On a phone, a tablet, or a narrow window the sidebar folds
away. Click the **☰** menu button to open it, or widen the browser window.
