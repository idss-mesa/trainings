# Find a MESA working group and how to join it

!!! warning "Draft: not yet validated"
    These steps were followed once on the live portal on 2026-11-16, by the AI
    assistant that drafted them. **Request to Join** was not clicked. No person has yet followed them, so a screen may
    differ. [Tell us what you find](https://github.com/idss-mesa/trainings/issues).

**You will:** know which MESA working groups exist, what one of them is for, and what
it asks of people who want to join.

**Time:** 5 minutes. **You need:** to be [signed in to the MESA Portal](sign-in.md).

## Steps

1. In the sidebar, click **Working Groups**.

    You should see: a card for each group, with its name, a short description, its
    number of members, and a few tags. The header counts the groups.

2. In the search box, type a word from your field, for example `synthesis`.

    You should see: only the cards whose name, description, or tags hold that word.
    On the day this was written, `synthesis` left two of seven.

3. Clear the search box.

    You should see: every card again.

4. On a card that interests you, click **View details**.

    You should see: the group's own page, with its name in the header and a
    **Request to Join** button.

5. Read **About This Working Group**.

    You should see: what the group studies, who leads it, and often the Data Store
    folder where it keeps shared data, such as a folder under `/iplant/home/shared/`.

6. Scroll to **Requirements to Join** and read it.

    You should see: who the group is for, and how requests are decided. Do not click
    **Request to Join** unless you fit and mean to take part: the request goes to the
    group's owners.

## Check it worked

You can name one group, say in a sentence what it is for, and say whether its
requirements fit you. Your **Home** page lists the groups you belong to under **My
Working Groups**; until you have joined one it reads *You're not in any working groups
yet*.

## If it didn't

**The search leaves no cards.** No group uses that word. Try a broader one, or clear
the box and read the tags on the cards.

**A group's page has no Request to Join button.** You may already be a member, or have
a request waiting. Look for the group under **My Working Groups** on **Home**. This
case was not seen when the tutorial was written.
